What the new planning rules mean for renters in Dublin

Sample article written for this prototype. The text below is placeholder content, not Irish Times reporting.

Photo: Unsplash (sample image)

Sample Author··4 MIN READ·🎧 Listen

New planning rules due to come into force in January are aimed mainly at new developments, according to the department’s published guidance. Builders will be asked to include a minimum share of long-term rental homes in larger schemes.

For people already renting, the guidance says existing tenancies will keep their current terms. Housing groups have welcomed the direction but say the details on enforcement are still thin.

Developers, for their part, argue that the new requirements could slow down some projects that are already in planning. The department says a review will take place after the first year.

Rent levels themselves are not directly set by the new rules. Economists quoted in earlier coverage expect any effect on prices to be gradual, and to depend on how many new homes are actually built.

A public consultation on the guidance remains open until the end of the month.
